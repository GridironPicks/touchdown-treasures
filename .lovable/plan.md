# Redraw the mascot crests to match your reference sheets

## Why they look different

The 62 crests I added were drawn fresh in the app's navy / silver / green colors, and the characters were made original on purpose so nothing copies a movie character. That is why they read softer and cooler than your examples, which are bold red-and-black esports emblems with heavy outlines and gritty shading.

## What changes

Redraw the artwork in the reference style: bold red/crimson, deep black, and white, thick outline, aggressive angular shapes, dramatic shading, tight circular or shield-style badge framing. Characters stay original (no Freddy, Jason, Predator, etc.) but the attitude and finish match your sheets.

## Step 1 - four samples

I redraw four crests first, one from each group, so you can confirm the look before I commit to all 62:

- Beasts: Gorilla
- Warriors: Spartan
- Sci-Fi & Gridiron: Cyber Wolf
- Horror: Reaper

You review them in chat. If they are right, I continue. If not, I adjust the style and show four again.

## Step 2 - the rest

Once approved, I redraw the remaining 58 in the same style and replace the current images. Names, groups, and anyone's already-chosen mascot stay exactly as they are - only the pictures change.
